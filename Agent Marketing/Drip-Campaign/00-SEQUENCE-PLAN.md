# Original build: 6 Emails, 15 Days

**Audience:** DFW real estate agents (works for traditional, new, and investment-brokerage agents)
**Goal:** book on Jeanne Claire's calendar (https://calendar.app.google/HYZr5JW9zkUSz92MA) or apply (joinmyers.com)
**Voice:** charter voice, Title Case headings, no income promises, no competitor names. Every claim traces to the Myers Packet or verified research.

## Two tracks, one sequence

The list must be split before sending, because the opening argument differs:

- **Track A (investment-brokerage agents):** start with `01-Day0-The-Math.txt`. The 90% is devastating against a 20-30% margin split.
- **Track B (traditional agents):** start with `01B-Day0-Traditional-Agents.txt`. They already keep most of their commission, so 90% carries no weight. Their pain is pipeline: they pay for leads and hunt for every closing. Lead with deal flow, both sides plus keeping traditional listings, the investor ladder, and no caps/royalty/desk fees.

Emails 2-6 are shared by both tracks. Email 2 (the spread) teaches Track B the model and reminds Track A why their split stings. From Email 3 on, the story, downline, filter, and 9-word close work identically.

## Why this mix beats all-HTML
Plain-text emails land in Primary, read as 1:1, and get replies; HTML emails carry the two ideas that need to be SEEN (the spread, the downline). Alternating also varies the fingerprint, which helps deliverability. Both HTML emails are single self-contained files, no images, no attachments, so they load in any browser and paste clean.

- **Exit conditions:** booking, application, reply, or unsubscribe. Never keep sending after a reply.
- **Re-entry:** eligible again after 90 days with a fresh Email 1 variant.
- **Suppression:** current Myers agents, anyone already in the funnel board, anyone Jeanne has messaged 1:1 this month.

## Send rules (spam avoidance)
- Send from Jeanne Claire's real address, not a marketing alias.
- Small batches (25-50/day), personalized first line where possible.
- No attachments, no link shorteners, only two domains linked (calendar.app.google, joinmyers.com).
- Plain-text emails: keep them truly plain. No images, no formatting.

## Benchmarks (recruiting cold/warm nurture)
Open 25-40% (plain text runs higher) · Click 3-7% · Reply 2-5% on emails 1 and 6 · Booked+applied 2-5% of list over the full sequence. Review weekly for the first month.
