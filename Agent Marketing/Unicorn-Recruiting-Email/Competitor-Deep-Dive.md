## What their recruiting messaging promises (their words)
- New Western: "Use Our Money to Acquire Properties" · "unmatched deal flow" · "close your first transaction in 60 days or less" · "$93,000 average, top performers exceed $450,000"
- NetWorth: "Performance-Powered Pay – first-year agents average $90K, with no ceiling" · "powerful 5-week training program" · "no prior experience needed (we actually prefer it that way!)"
- Prophet: "100% commission-based with uncapped income" · "paid weekly" · "agents frequently sell 3-5X as many properties as standard realtors"
- RFP: "unlimited earning potential" · "equity opportunities for key roles" · "Treats the role like you own the business"

**Pattern:** they all sell *access* (deal flow, uncapped income, speed to first check). None of them can sell *ownership*. That lane is empty.

## Where their agents hurt (documented, with receipts)
1. **The margin math.** NetWorth agents keep 20–30% of the spread — their own agents say so publicly. New Western review: "You have to sell about 10+ houses a month to make $20K." At low volume this business feels like 12–25%. (Jeanne's insider estimate: max ~20/34%, low-volume 12–25% — consistent with, but not proven by, the public record.)
2. **The grind.** New Western's own FAQ: "lead generation and cold calling are essential." Reviews: "pound the phones... a bit cutthroat," "for every 2 people coming in, there are 2 headed out," "Wolf of Wall Street mentality."
3. **The ceiling.** New Western agents cannot touch retail transactions and invest only "within our guidelines." No one in the set has revenue share. The ladder tops out at manager — not owner.
4. **Trust drag.** Investor-side distrust documented (BiggerPockets: inflated valuations, pressure tactics at NetWorth; Prophet reviews allege coached scarcity tactics). Myers' both-sides model + real closings are the antidote.

## Messaging doctrine vs. this set
- **Mirror their language, then out-promise structurally:** they say "deal flow" — we say "deal flow from day one, *and the deal can be yours*." They say "uncapped" — we say "uncapped, five ways."
- **Never name competitors in public content.** In 1:1 email, name only what's sourced (NetWorth's 20/30 is safe to cite as "reported by their own agents"). New Western/Prophet/RFP splits: say "not published."
- **Don't fight New Western on scale** ($17B, largest US buyer — documented). Fight on *economics and ownership*.
- **Their recruits are pre-qualified for us.** Anyone who survived cold-call culture at 20-30% of margin already has the exact skills Myers pays 80% for. Message: "You've already proven you can do the hard part. You're just doing it for someone else's balance sheet."
