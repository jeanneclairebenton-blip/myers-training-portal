# Myers 14-Day Recruiting Sprint — 8 Signed Licensed Agents

**Goal:** 8 licensed agents with sponsorship transferred to Myers in TREC REALM by Day 14.
**Operator:** Jeanne, 4+ hrs/day, plus activated current agents. No warm pipeline. No ad budget assumed.

---

## The Math (why the plan looks like this)

Industry benchmark: ~60–75 qualified contacts per signed agent, and average time-to-fill is ~44 days. You have 14. That means:

- **Cold outreach alone will not get you 8.** 8 hires × 60–75 contacts = 480–600 qualified touches — at the very edge of what one person can do in 12 working days (~50/day).
- **Referrals convert 3–5x better** and drive 20–30% of brokerage hires. Your current agents are the single highest-leverage asset you have.
- **Speed-to-sign is your friend:** TREC sponsorship changes happen in REALM in hours, not weeks. An agent who says yes on a Tuesday call can be a Myers agent by Tuesday night. Compress every step.

**Working funnel target:** 600 touches → ~90 conversations → ~30 sit-downs → 8–10 signs.
Referrals should shortcut this: every referral sit-down is worth ~4 cold ones.

---

## Who To Target (ranked by speed to signature)

1. **Referrals from current Myers agents** — highest conversion. Every agent knows 5+ licensed agents who are struggling.
2. **Inactive Texas licensees in DFW** — they *cannot practice at all* without a sponsoring broker. Myers isn't asking them to leave anything; it's an activation offer. Findable via TREC License Holder Search (filter: inactive, DFW counties). Fastest signatures in the pool. *Quality risk: screen for commitment — inactive often means checked-out.*
3. **Licensed agents 0–24 months in, at big-box brokerages** — paying desk fees on 50–70% splits with zero leads. Most are pre-failure and know it. Findable via: Zillow/Realtor.com agent profiles with 0–3 sales, DFW agent Facebook groups, Instagram #dfwrealtor accounts with low listing activity, new-licensee congratulation posts.
4. **Licensed wholesalers / investor-adjacent agents** — already speak the Myers language; the "licensed investor-agent" identity is built for them. Findable in DFW REIA groups, investor meetup attendee lists, wholesaling Facebook groups.

**Do not chase producing agents at good brokerages.** They take 60–90 days and won't move in your window.

---

## The Pitch (fix this BEFORE outreach — Day 0)

Two credibility problems in the current materials will kill conversions with licensed agents:

1. **Drop "highest commission in the country."** 100%-commission brokerages exist in DFW; any licensed agent knows this. One false-sounding claim discredits everything after it. **Lead with the true differentiator instead: deal flow.** Nobody else hands a new agent a pipeline of off-market deals on Day 1. The pitch is: *"90% payout is the floor. The reason agents join is the deals we put in front of you."*
2. **Get proof ready.** Before Day 2, collect from your current roster: 2–3 real agent stories with real numbers (first-deal timeline, actual commission check), screenshots of the deal pipeline, and the 200+ Google reviews link. Licensed agents will verify; sharp ones distrust unverifiable math like "$15K average commission."

**30-second pitch (use everywhere):**
> "Myers is a DFW investor-brokerage — 3,000+ transactions. Agents keep 90% and get access to our off-market deal pipeline from day one, so you're not cold-calling for listings. Our agents learn to flip and hold their own properties too. Fifteen-minute call to see if it fits?"

## Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 0–1 (Wed–Thu): Build the machine
- Fix pitch per above. Write one offer one-pager (split, deal flow, training, path to investing, what it costs the agent — be explicit about any fees).
- Set up a same-day sign flow: sit-down → offer → REALM sponsorship request initiated on the spot. Rehearse it once.
- Pull target lists: TREC inactive licensees (DFW counties), 50 low-production agent profiles from Zillow/Realtor.com, join 3–5 DFW agent Facebook groups.
- Set up simple pipeline tracking in Zoho CRM (stages: Touched → Replied → Sit-down → Offer → Signed).
- **Announce internally: referral bounty.** If budget allows anything, $500–$1,500 per signed agent paid to the referring agent (structured referral programs drive 20–30% of hires). If zero budget: recognition + revenue-share positioning ("they join your downline").

### Day 2 (Fri): Referral blitz — the most important day
- Call every current Myers agent personally. Script below. Ask each for **5 names of licensed agents** — struggling, new, inactive, or unhappy. Get intros, not just names ("text them now and copy me in").
- Every intro gets contacted **within 1 hour** (1-hour response makes qualification ~7x more likely).
- Have every agent post their own "why I joined Myers" story this week using the welcome-card/content-desk assets already built.
- Evening: first 25 cold DMs to list.

### Days 3–4 (Sat–Sun): Volume begins
- 50 touches/day: DMs (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn) + calls to inactive licensees (phone numbers via TREC records/skip trace — the training materials say Myers already does skip tracing).
- Post in DFW agent Facebook groups (value post, not a job ad — e.g., "what a double-close actually pays an agent" breakdown).
- Book sit-downs for Mon–Wed. Offer 15-min Zoom or in-office. Always propose two specific times.

### Days 5–7 (Mon–Wed): First closes
- Morning: sit-downs (target 3–4/day). Same-day REALM signing for every yes.
- Afternoon: 40–50 new touches + follow-ups (every non-responder gets touch #2; it takes 3–5 touches to get a reply).
- **Checkpoint Day 7: you need 2–3 signed.** If at zero, the bottleneck is either list quality (switch harder to inactive licensees) or the sit-down (record one, review it).
- Announce a **Deal Flow Night** for Day 10 or 11: 90 minutes at the office — live look at the current off-market pipeline, one agent tells their first-deal story, Q&A, applications on site. Every prospect who hasn't signed gets invited. Every current agent brings one licensed guest.

### Days 8–9 (Thu–Fri): Second referral pass + event fill
- Re-call current agents: "Who else? Your first intro booked a sit-down." Momentum makes the second ask easier.
- Push all warm-but-undecided prospects to Deal Flow Night. Fence-sitters convert in rooms, not in DMs — belonging and social proof do what pitches can't.
- Keep 40 touches/day running underneath.

### Day 10–11: Deal Flow Night + close
- Run the event. Make joining frictionless: laptops open, REALM requests initiated on site.
- Next morning: call every attendee within 12 hours. Direct ask.

### Days 12–14: Close everything
- No new outreach after Day 12. Pure follow-up and closing.
- Every open sit-down gets a direct, deadline-honest close: "Our next onboarding cohort starts Monday — I'd like you in it." (True urgency — a real cohort date — not manufactured scarcity.)
- Final referral sweep of anyone who signed during the sprint: new joiners are your hottest referrers ("who did you get licensed with?").

## Scripts

**Referral ask (call to current agents):**
> "I'm bringing on 8 agents this month and I'd rather build this team from people you vouch for than strangers. Who do you know with a license who isn't getting what they need — someone new, someone stuck at a big brokerage, someone whose license went inactive? … Would you text them right now and connect us?"

**Cold DM (new/struggling licensed agent):**
> "Hey [name] — saw you're licensed in DFW. Quick question: is your current brokerage actually handing you deals, or just training and fees? Myers is an investor-brokerage (3,000+ DFW deals) — agents keep 90% and work our off-market pipeline from day one. Worth a 15-min call this week?"

**Inactive licensee (call/text):**
> "Hi [name], this is Jeanne with Myers Home Buyers in DFW. Your license shows inactive — I'm reaching out because we sponsor agents and put them straight into our off-market deal pipeline, so reactivating actually pays from month one instead of costing you. Got 15 minutes this week?"

**Sit-down structure (30 min):** their story first (10 min — listen for the wound: no leads, no support, fees) → Myers model + one real agent example with numbers (10 min) → show the live deal pipeline (5 min) → direct ask + REALM on the spot (5 min).

## Honest Assessment

- **This target is aggressive.** Benchmark time-to-fill is 44 days; you have 14 with no pipeline. 8 is reachable *only* if the referral channel produces 4+ of them and inactive licensees (lowest friction) produce 2–3. If Day 7 lands at 2–3 signed, you're on pace. A realistic floor if execution is good but referrals underdeliver: 4–5 signed, with the rest closing in week 3–4 — and you'll have built the standing pipeline the project mission calls for.
- **Vet while you sprint.** 8 warm bodies who churn in 60 days is worse than 5 who produce. Keep the "who succeeds here" filter: commission-tolerant, coachable, genuinely wants the investor path.
- **After the sprint,** the engine to keep: weekly agent-referral ask, monthly Deal Flow Night, always-on content from the content desk, and speed-to-lead discipline on every inbound.
