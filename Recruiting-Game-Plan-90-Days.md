# Myers 90-Day Recruiting Game Plan — Zero Ad Budget

**Goal you set:** 20 hired / 14 kept in 90 days.
**Budget:** $0 paid ads.
**Companion docs:** `Job-Posts-2026.md` (the posts + posting ops) and `14-Day-Recruiting-Sprint.md` (licensed-agent outbound sprint — this plan absorbs it).

## 1. The math, straight

Your own Recruitment Model priced 20 hires at ~$30,000 of ad spend (2,000 applicants → 200 interviews → 20 hired → 14 kept). You've removed the fuel but kept the destination. Here's what the evidence says $0 buys:

- **Free job boards:** Your organic Indeed history: 126 applicants → 2 hires (no spend). Optimized posts (pay transparency, 5-word titles, Monday posting, 10-day reposts — all now built) should beat that meaningfully, but a realistic zero-budget board yield is **4–7 hires per quarter**, not 15.
- **Referrals:** Drive 20–30% of brokerage hires industry-wide and convert 3–5x better than cold. Your one tracked referral hire had a 100% keep rate. With ~14–17 current agents each knowing 5+ licensed agents, this is your single biggest free asset: **5–8 hires** if worked weekly, every week.
- **Outbound (TREC inactive licensees + struggling new agents):** Pure labor. Benchmark ~60–75 qualified contacts per hire. At 40–50 touches/day, 5 days/week: **4–6 hires per quarter**.
- **Events (Deal Flow Nights):** Converters for fence-sitters, not a standalone source: **+1–2**.

**Honest total: 12–18 hires in 90 days at $0, with 20 possible only if referrals overperform.** One more data point before you accept that gap: your own 2024 numbers show Wizehire delivered applicants at $2–6 each and hires at ~$178–375. Roughly **$500/month ($1,500 total) would likely close the entire gap to 20** — cheaper than the labor cost of the extra outbound hours it replaces. Decision is yours; the plan below assumes $0. *(Myers internal evidence + Recruiting Insight 2026.)*

Also fix before Day 1: **the "kept" side.** Your model keeps 14 of 20 (70%), but agent feedback in your files shows promised mentorship and deal flow haven't always materialized. Recruiting harder into a leaky bucket wastes every hour of this plan. Gallup: 42% of turnover is preventable; half of quitters cite their manager. The retention fixes are in §6.

## 2. The engine — four channels, one weekly rhythm

### Channel 1: Referrals (highest priority, ~40% of hires)

- **Every Tuesday:** referral ask to every current agent. Rotate who you call so each agent hears from you personally at least every 3 weeks. Script is in the sprint doc. Ask for intros ("text them now, copy me"), not names.
- **Bounty:** with $0 cash, use what the packet already sells — recognition + revenue share. "They join your downline; Level 1 pays you 25% of the pool credit on their production, monthly, forever." That is a real, permanent referral incentive most brokerages can't match. Make it explicit at every ask.
- **New-hire sweep:** within 7 days of anyone signing, ask "who did you get licensed with?" New joiners are the hottest referrers.
- **Target: 2 referral intros/week minimum.** Below that two weeks running, the ask or the incentive isn't landing — diagnose in Friday review.

### Channel 2: Job posts (free tier, ~30% of hires)

Fully specified in `Job-Posts-2026.md`. Non-negotiables: Zoho→Indeed Apply integration verified ON; post Mondays; 3 free Indeed slots always full; repost every 10–14 days; LinkedIn free slot runs Post B; screen and call every qualified applicant within one business day.

### Channel 3: Outbound (~25% of hires)

- **Lists:** TREC License Holder Search — inactive licensees in Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, Denton counties (activation offer: they can't practice without a sponsor; you're not asking them to leave anything). Plus low-production agents (0–3 sales) from Zillow/Realtor.com profiles, DFW agent Facebook groups, #dfwrealtor Instagram.
- **Volume:** 40 touches/day, Mon–Fri (DMs + calls + texts). Every non-responder gets touches 2 and 3 across 10 days — 3–5 touches is where replies happen.
- **Do not chase producing agents at good brokerages** (60–90 day cycles; wrong window).

### Channel 4: Deal Flow Night (monthly, converts the undecided)

Last Thursday of each month, 90 minutes, Addison office: live look at the current off-market pipeline, one agent tells their first-deal story with the real check, Q&A, REALM sponsorship initiated on-site for anyone ready. Every warm-but-undecided prospect from all three channels gets invited; every current agent brings one licensed guest. Fence-sitters convert in rooms — belonging and social proof do what DMs can't. *(Established persuasion research: social proof + in-group belonging; and your sprint doc's event design.)*

## 3. Weekly operating rhythm

| Day | Block (2–4 hrs) |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Post/refresh job posts (AM — freshness window). Process weekend applicants; every qualified one called same day. 20 outbound touches. |
| **Tue** | **Referral day:** 5+ agent calls with the ask. 1-hr response on every intro. Interviews PM. |
| **Wed** | 40 outbound touches. Interviews. Follow-up pass (touch 2/3 on all non-responders). |
| **Thu** | Interviews (stack them — target 10–12/week total). 20 touches. Monthly: Deal Flow Night. |
| **Fri** | **Scoreboard review** (§5). Second referral pass on agents who gave intros. Plan Monday's posts. Social proof post from the content desk (agent story/first check). |

Speed rules, always on: **reply to every applicant/intro within 1 hour during work hours; interview within 48 hours of first call; offer within 24 hours of a good interview; REALM initiated same-day on every yes.** Speed is a free conversion multiplier — the only one you have at $0. *(Recruiting Insight 2026: 1-hour response ≈ 7x qualification.)*

## 4. The offer candidates hear (say it the same way everywhere)

> "Myers is a DFW investor-brokerage — 3,000+ transactions. Agents keep 90% of the commission pool (80% direct + 10% agent revenue share) and work our off-market deal pipeline from day one, so you're not cold-calling for listings. You'll learn to flip and hold your own properties. It's 100% commission and we're honest about the ramp. Fifteen minutes to see if it fits?"

**Never say:** "highest commission in the country/industry" (verifiably false — 100% shops exist; one false claim poisons everything after it), any income figure you can't produce a check for, or "guaranteed" anything.

**Proof pack (assemble Week 1, use in every interview and event):** 2–3 real agent stories with real numbers and timelines, pipeline screenshots, Google reviews link, the fee list ($150/mo, $100/transaction, $0 desk). Candidates in 2026 assume exaggeration; documents beat adjectives. *(Gallup: stability/security top-4 at 54%; Appcast 2026: transparency converts.)*

## 5. Scoreboard (Zoho, reviewed every Friday)

Weekly minimums to stay on pace for the honest 12–18 (and 20 if referrals fire):

| Metric | Weekly floor |
|---|---|
| New applicants (boards) | 40 |
| Referral intros | 2 |
| Outbound touches | 160 |
| Interviews held | 10 |
| Offers | 2 |
| Signed/hired | 1–2 |
| Response time (median) | < 1 hour |

Diagnosis rules: applicants low → posts (title/pay line) or posting cadence; interviews low → screening or speed; offers low → interview quality; acceptances low → offer/positioning; keep low → onboarding (§6). Change **one variable at a time**, exactly as your Feb-2024 workbook discipline did.

Pull stats from Zoho, never from board dashboards (your own documented lesson).

## 6. Retention: protect the "14 kept"

1. **First 30 days scripted:** every new agent gets a named mentor, a first-deal target date, and the onboarding modules with checkpoints. The #1 credibility gap in your own files is promised support that didn't materialize — this is where "kept" is won.
2. **First check ceremony:** recognize every first closed deal publicly (Slack + Deal Flow Night). Recognition measurably drives retention (Gallup).
3. **30/60/90 stay-conversations:** 15 minutes, three questions — what did we promise you, what's actually happening, what's in your pipeline. 42% of turnover is preventable and shows up in these conversations first (Gallup).
4. **Deal-flow honesty:** if company-gen deal volume can't feed every new SA, cap hiring pace to deal flow or say so in onboarding. Agents quitting over thin pipelines is your recorded past — don't re-run it at 2x scale.
