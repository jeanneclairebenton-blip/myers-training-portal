## 1. The Identity (the one sentence)

> **Myers is where agents become investors.**

You already own this line — it's on your own graphic ("Where Agents Become Investors.png"). This charter makes it official.

**The full promise (say a version of this constantly):**
"At Myers, you don't work for a brokerage — you build your own business with one. Flips, rentals, deals, your own downline. You keep 90% of the commission pool (80% direct + 10% revenue share), you work real deal flow from day one, and you get an AI-powered lead engine, weekly live classes with active investors, and 1:1 mentorship to your first closed deal. We bring the team, the training, and the deals. You build the empire."

**Why this identity and not another:** it's the only claim in your market that is simultaneously (a) true and provable with your own agents, (b) emotionally bigger than a commission split, and (c) something eXp, KW, and Compass structurally cannot copy — they don't buy houses. Commission splits attract shoppers; identity attracts believers.

## 2. Voice (how every post sounds)

- Owner language, never employee language. Agents "build," "close," "own," "invest." Never "work for us," "our staff," "join our team of employees."
- Trendy, catchy, funny, engaging. Confident, a little playful. Never corporate, never cheesy, never desperate ("WE'RE HIRING!!! 🔥🔥").
- Numbers over adjectives. "Cynthia's flip: bought $XXX, sold $XXX" beats "amazing opportunity."
- Short. Under 300 words, always.

## 3. The four proof points (every follower should absorb these within 2 weeks of following)

1. **Ownership** — agents build their own business; Myers celebrates it.
2. **The money** — 80% + 10% pool = 90% back to agents; 20%→60% ladder; $0 desk fees; fees stated plainly ($150/mo, $100/txn).
3. **The deals** — off-market deal flow + Carrot AI lead engine from day one (stated as fact, never as "biggest pipeline").
4. **The people** — real agents, real checks, real training, a team that actually likes each other.

## 4. One promise, three translations

| Audience | What they're afraid of | What they hear from Myers |
|---|---|---|
| **New agents** | Starving the first year | "You won't cold-call into the void. Deal flow, live classes, and a mentor until your first check." |
| **Experienced agents** | Fees + no support + capped income | "Keep 90%. Stop buying your own leads. Add flips, rentals, and rev-share on top of commissions." |
| **Team leads / brokerages** | Plateaued, carrying everyone | "Plug your whole team into our pipeline. Six-level revenue share — you get paid on everyone you bring." |

## 5. Banned claims (these kill trust — one false line poisons everything after it)

- ❌ "Highest commission in the industry/country" (falsifiable — 100% shops exist)
- ❌ Any income figure you can't produce a real check for
- ❌ "Biggest / best off-market pipeline" (competitors are bigger; state deal flow as fact only)
- ❌ "Guaranteed" anything
- ❌ "$10K minimum margin" / per-deal income floors (unconfirmed internally)
- ✅ Allowed: "industry-leading commission structure," real deals with real numbers, "3,000+ DFW transactions"
