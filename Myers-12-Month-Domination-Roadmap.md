# Owning the Agent-Investor Market: 12-Month Roadmap
### August 2026 – July 2027 · Companion to the Agent-Investor Manifesto
*Every target below is a number we can measure on the quarterly dashboard. Assumes current capacity: ~150–180 acquisitions/year, ~47% contract close rate (Zoho, July 2026).*

---

## The one-sentence strategy
Own the **category name** first (cheap, fast, nobody contests it), dominate the **talent funnel** second (mindshare + the CE front door), and scale **deal share** last (capital-paced, 1.2% → 3%).

---

## PHASE 1 — OWN THE CLAIM (Months 1–3: Aug–Oct 2026)
**Goal: "agent-investor" becomes a phrase people in DFW real estate repeat. 12+ recruit conversations/month from content.**

What ships:
1. **Ratify the Manifesto** (Week 1). Every post, ad, and email inherits its language rules from day one.
2. **Category content blitz** — the 5 weekly calendar slots reorient around one job: teach the market the phrase *agent-investor*. Pillars: "the ladder" stories (real agents, real rungs), incentive-alignment posts ("their model needs you to stay an agent; ours needs you to graduate"), flip-becomes-listing proof, portfolio-career hooks ("keep the music career — add doors").
3. **Unicorn campaign wave 2** — the finished email + visuals go to the competitor-agent and "already-investing-badly" lists (DFW_Wholesale_Brokerage_Heavy_Hitters + newly-licensed lists already in the folder). Booking link finally dropped in.
4. **Proof engine starts** — film/capture every first check, first deal, first door bought. Raw > polished. This library is Phase 2's fuel.
5. **Legal check** — quietly verify the KW-team funding loophole. If clear, "the only brokerage" language unlocks for Phase 2.

Scoreboard at Oct 31: baseline dashboard live · ≥36 booked recruiting conversations · ≥6 signed agents · "agent-investor" appearing in comments/DMs unprompted.

## PHASE 2 — OWN THE FUNNEL (Months 3–9: Oct 2026–Apr 2027)
**Goal: a machine that produces candidates while we sleep. The CE front door opens.**

What ships:
1. **The CE front door** (gated on the TREC research landing — go/no-go within 30 days of it). If GO: file provider + course applications immediately; target first TREC-credit investment class inside this phase. Every licensed agent in Texas needs 18 CE hours every 2 years — we become where DFW agents *choose* to spend them, and every classroom seat is a warm recruit who came to us.
2. **Myers University goes public-facing** — Orientation Units A–E stay internal; a public "Intro to the Agent-Investor Career" track becomes the top-of-funnel lead magnet (free, email-gated).
3. **Nurture sequences** — every CE attendee, class registrant, and Unicorn reply enters a drip built on the alignment message. No lead dies in a spreadsheet again.
4. **Referral flywheel** — agents already earn on the 6-level rev share; we arm them with shareable assets so recruiting becomes something agents do *to build their own tree*, not a favor to Myers.
5. **SEO/search capture** — own "become a real estate investor DFW," "investor agent Dallas," "real estate acquisitions job DFW." The searches already happen; New Western currently eats them.

Scoreboard at Apr 30: CE decision executed · ≥100 funnel entries/month · ≥4 signed agents/month · agent headcount +25–30 vs. today · cost-per-recruit known.

## PHASE 3 — SCALE THE SHARE (Months 9–12: Apr–Jul 2027)
**Goal: convert talent dominance into deal share. 1.2% → 2%+ of DFW investor purchases.**

What ships:
1. **Capacity math** — every point of deal share ≈ +140 purchases/year. Phase 3 opens with a capital-and-ops review: how many more acquisition agents can we feed? Fix the 47% close rate before buying more contracts (each point of close-rate improvement is free volume).
2. **County expansion by data** — Zoho shows Collin + Denton are underweight vs. Dallas/Tarrant; content and recruiting get geo-targeted there.
3. **Alumni investor network** — agents who graduated to investor become the loudest proof and the deal-flow multiplier. Their stories headline everything.
4. **Quarterly dashboard cadence locked** — investor-purchase share, MLS-side share, headcount share, reviewed in leadership every quarter, forever.

Scoreboard at Jul 31, 2027: 220+ purchases/year run-rate · ≥2% DFW investor-purchase share · 50+ agents · ≥3 agents graduated to investor in the trailing year (the number the whole brand rests on).

## What can kill this (watch list)
- **Data blindness:** Zoho sales-side modules still locked; Amount fields blank on many deals. Fix in Phase 1 or the dashboard lies.
- **Overclaiming:** one "passive income" or unverifiable "only/highest" claim can burn the credibility the category play depends on. The Manifesto's language rules are load-bearing.
- **KW + Stone Point:** PE money could fund a capital product. Quarterly recheck; 90 days' notice is enough to counter-position ("they rent you capital; we built the ladder").
- **Capacity mirage:** recruiting ahead of deal flow breaks the promise to new agents. Talent funnel throttles to acquisition capacity, not the other way around.
