MYERS LMS ICON SETS
Library base: Lucide (lucide.dev), ISC license, free for commercial use. Poppins + gold branding.
Style rule: pick ONE folder per surface and never mix sets side by side.

1-Classic-Line ... flat brand gold #C9972C, 2px stroke. Cleanest, smallest, best at tiny sizes (under 18px).
2-Duotone-Gold ... gradient stroke (#E8BC5E to #A87B1F) + soft gold fills. Premium feel for cards and menus.
3-Dynamic-Gold ... duotone base + motion accents (speed lines, sparks, typing dots, alarm rings,
                   ground ripple, notification dot, keyhole, dashed route). The signature set:
                   use for feature moments like the dashboard, badges, and celebrations.

RECOMMENDED USE
Tab bars / tiny UI ........ 1-Classic-Line
Cards, menus, lists ....... 2-Duotone-Gold
Dashboard, badges, hero ... 3-Dynamic-Gold

Inactive state: change strokes/fills to gray #6B6B6B (fills at ~8% opacity).
