# The Agent-Investor Manifesto
### Myers' definition of its place in the market — for ratification alongside the Brand Charter
*Drafted July 19, 2026. Built entirely on claims verified in the KW-vs-Myers Market Position Report.*

---

## 1. The category we claim

Every brokerage in DFW sells one of two things: a place to hang your license, or a story about wealth. Myers sells neither. Myers created a job that didn't exist:

# **The Agent-Investor.**

An agent-investor is a licensed real estate professional who acquires property with company capital, earns like an owner, and graduates into ownership. Not an agent who dabbles in investing. Not an investor who happens to hold a license. A defined career with a defined ladder and a defined endpoint: **your own portfolio.**

Myers doesn't compete in the brokerage category. Myers **is** the agent-investor category — and the rest of the market proves it by their absence:

- The big franchise brokerages **sell the story**: paid wealth classes at $199–$1,000/month, plus profit-share programs that average a few hundred dollars per agent per year. They teach you to invest. You bring the money. *(Verified against their own published pages, July 2026.)*
- The investment brokerages **fund the deal but cap the agent**: their acquisition agents source inventory for the company to resell, forever. There is no ladder. The agent's ceiling is "good sourcer."
- **Myers funds the deal AND builds the investor.** Company capital on every acquisition. Industry-leading economics — 90% of revenue back to agents (80% commission + 10% revenue-share pool). A ladder that runs Sales Agent → Acquisitions → Team Lead → Investor, with the door to investing open at any stage. Free weekly live classes with active investors. 1:1 mentorship to your first deal. And both sides of the business — investment acquisitions *and* traditional listings — under one roof.

## 2. The positioning statement

> **For agents who got licensed to build wealth — not to grind commissions forever — Myers is the brokerage built to turn agents into investors: we fund your acquisitions with our money, return 90% of revenue to our agents, and walk you up a ladder that ends in ownership.**

One sentence version for ads and bios:
> **Myers. Where agents become investors.**

## 3. The incentive-alignment truth (our sharpest weapon)

Other brokerages profit most when you stay an agent forever — every commission you split, every month of fees, every class they sell you. **Myers only scales if you actually become an investor.** We are the only model in this market whose business breaks if the promise is broken. Alignment is the argument no competitor can copy without rebuilding their company.

## 4. Proof points (all sourced, all publishable)

1. **90% of revenue back to agents** — 80% commission + 10% revenue-share pool. Say "industry-leading." Never say "highest in the industry."
2. **Company-funded acquisitions** — Myers funds every purchase; acquisition agents buy on Myers' behalf. 150–180 houses per year, every year, for the last three years (Zoho, July 2026).
3. **The ladder** — SA 20% → Acquisitions 60% → Team Lead 50% + 6-level rev share → Investor. Include verbatim: *agents can become investors at any stage.*
4. **Your brand, powered by Myers** — Carrot partnership: every agent gets their own AI-driven website + CRM.
5. **Free weekly live classes with active investors** — the thing others charge up to $12,000/year for, included.
6. **1:1 mentorship to your first closed deal.**
7. Full brokerage support, transparent costs ($150/mo, $100/txn, $0 desk) — stated plainly, at the bottom, always.

## 5. Language rules (non-negotiable)

- **Never name competitors** in published assets. Comparisons use anonymized "Brokerage A/B" framing only.
- Say **"built to turn agents into investors"** — not "the only brokerage that…" — until the team-level funding loophole check clears. Same punch, zero legal exposure. (If the check clears, "only" unlocks.)
- **Never "passive income."** Agent income is earned. Say "ownership income," "equity," "wealth you keep."
- Never invent or exaggerate income claims; per-deal and margin numbers come from the workbook or don't get used.
- Both investment and traditional real estate get mentioned; the flip-becomes-listing cycle is part of the story.
- Voice per Brand Charter: trendy, funny, ownership-proud — never corporate.

## 6. What "winning the market" means (and the two scoreboards)

We dominate the **talent market** (every DFW agent who wants to own property — mindshare, measurable via agent-headcount share and inbound recruits) so we can grow the **deal market** (share of DFW investor purchases — currently ~1.2%, capital-constrained by design). Talent share is the lead domino. When any DFW agent thinks "I want to invest," the reflex must be **Myers**. The quarterly dashboard tracks both.
