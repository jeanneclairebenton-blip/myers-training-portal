Two posts, ~280 words each, standard searchable titles. Structure reflects Jeanne's strength hierarchy: **commission → growth ladder → Carrot AI personal brand → live investor classes → 1:1 mentorship**, with brokerage support and costs at the bottom (support is table stakes; costs defuse "too good to be true" right before the apply button). ## POST A — Sales Agent

**Location:** Addison, TX (DFW) · Full-time · **Pay field:** Commission-only, structure disclosed. *(Never tag "remote.")*

---

**At Myers Home Buyers, our mission is to transform real estate agents into real estate investors.** Industry-leading commission: **80% direct payout + 10% agent revenue-share pool — 90% of every commission dollar goes back to agents.**

**The job, in one line: our acquisitions team buys houses — you sell them.** Myers-funded, off-market inventory handed to you from day one, plus **traditional MLS deals** under the same agent-first split. Myers is a DFW brokerage with 3,000+ transactions closed.

**Your growth path is mapped from day one** — and it doesn't stop at "agent":

Sales Agent (20% payout) → Acquisitions (60%) → Team Lead (50% split + 6-level revenue share) → **Investor, buying your own properties.** *You can become an investor at any stage.* Five income streams along the way: commissions, wholesale fees, fix & flip, rentals, revenue share.

- **Your personal brand, powered by Myers:** through our Carrot partnership, your own website and CRM driven by AI — cutting-edge tech that builds *your* name from week one
- **Weekly live classes** taught by active investors and Myers leadership — not recorded webinars
- **1:1 mentorship with one target: your first closed deal**

**The honest part:** 100% commission, no base. Your first 30–60 days may pay little; we build your ramp around a first deal in 30 days. TX license required for commissions — active or in progress (we'll show you the fastest path).

**The essentials:** full brokerage support — contracts, compliance, transaction coordination. Costs, all of them: $150/mo tech fee, $100 per closed transaction, $0 desk fees.

**Apply in 2 minutes.** Qualified applicants get a 15-minute call within one business day. joinmyers.com

## Research brief: what makes a job post powerful (confidence levels stated)

**HIGH confidence — platform-scale data, replicated, current (2025–2026):**

1. **Short wins.** LinkedIn (millions of posts): <300 words converts +8.4% vs average; 301–600 words = −3.4%. Candidates decide in ~14 seconds, 50%+ on mobile.
2. **Pay transparency wins.** Appcast 2026: pay info → more clicks, higher apply rates, lower CPA. Indeed *requires* comp-structure disclosure for direct-sales roles.
3. **Standard, searchable, 4–6 word titles win.** Appcast 2026 + Indeed guidance. Invented titles ("Real Estate Investor Advisor") have no search volume and never get seen.
4. **Sub-5-minute applies win.** Appcast 2026. Resume + 3 screeners only.
5. **Monday posting + 10–14 day reposts.** ~20% of weekly applies land Monday; 50%+ of lifetime applications arrive in the first 10 days (Appcast 2026).

**HIGH-to-MODERATE — multi-source, survey-based:**

6. **Growth path is a top decision driver.** iHire 2025: 54.8% say clear advancement paths drive stay/join decisions; Gallup 2025: career acceleration is Gen Z's #1 differentiator (43% very important); 2026 brokerage-selection guides rank training/development as the top non-economic factor (Colibri, HousingWire, C21 guides). This is why the ladder now sits directly under the job description in both posts.
7. **Compensation, qualifications, and day-to-day duties are the content candidates rate most important** (LinkedIn). Posts lead with pay, one-line job clarity, three-line requirements.

**MODERATE — strong single-source:**

8. **Speed-to-contact ≈ 7x qualification** (Recruiting Insight 2026). The "call within one business day" promise is printed in the post as a differentiator.

**ESTABLISHED principles — older, well-replicated:**

9. **Realistic job previews reduce early turnover** (decades of I/O psychology). The "honest part" section is retention insurance — critical when NAR-reported data shows 62% of new agents earned under $10K in 2024.
10. **Future-identity framing drives career choice** (Gallup "do what I do best" 48%). The mission line sells becoming an investor, not taking a job.

**Excluded as unverifiable:** "$10K minimum margin/$6K floor" (contradicted by internal workbook avg of $9,550 — confirm enforced policy to add); "highest commission in the industry" (falsifiable superlative → packet's own "industry-leading"); pipeline-size superiority (larger competitors exist — Jeanne, July 2026).
