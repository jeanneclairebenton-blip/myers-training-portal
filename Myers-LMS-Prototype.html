MODULE 4.2 · NON-NEGOTIABLES
The Six Non-NegotiablesOne idea: the rules that protect every deal. 5 min video + 3 questions.
Narrated over visuals · transcript below
4:52
AFTER THE VIDEO: ONE IDEA PER TAPCARD 1 OF 3

The deposit is $5,000.

Not earnest money. Paid directly to Myers. Non-refundable, wire or cashier's check only.

The clock is 2 hours.

Winner signs and delivers the deposit within 2 hours of bidding closing, or the AA moves to the next buyer. Say it out loud up front.

Every deal is a double close.

Myers sells to the investor as a principal, through Myers' title company. A title policy protects everyone.

Check · Question 1 of 3
An investor says: "Will you take $3K as the deposit? I'm good for the rest."
Why: Non-Negotiable #1: investors bring their own EMD, and the deposit is $5,000, paid directly to Myers. No financing the deposit, no exceptions. This is a pass/fail rule: on the certificate check it requires 100%, not 80%.
Check · Question 2 of 3
Bidding closed an hour ago. The winner hasn't sent the wire or contract yet. What happens at the 2 hour mark?
Why: No delaying the deposit: wire plus contract are due within 2 hours of bidding closing, or the deal goes to the next buyer. Say it out loud up front so nobody is surprised.
Check · Question 3 of 3
An investor asks: "Can you just assign the contract to me?"
Why: Double close is Non-Negotiable #4, and it protects the investor too: a title policy is in place. This is also one of the Seven Questions every investor will test you with in the first conversation.
