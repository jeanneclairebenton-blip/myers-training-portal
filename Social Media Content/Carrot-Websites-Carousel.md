## PLATFORM MECHANICS (what's possible where)

- **Instagram:** mixed photo + video carousels fully supported (up to 20 slides, videos to ~60 sec each). Post exactly as planned. ✅
- **Facebook:** mixed media posts supported — attach photos + videos together. Order can be finicky in the composer; use Meta Business Suite to arrange slides. ✅
- **LinkedIn:** no native mixed carousels — carousels are PDF "document posts" (images only, no video). **The play:** post the same slides as a PDF doc (screenshots incl. a "▶ watch the scroll-through in comments" slide), then drop ONE scroll video as the first comment — or post the best scroll video as a native LinkedIn video the next day. Two posts, one asset. ✅

## HOW TO RECORD THE SCROLL VIDEOS (10 min each)

1. Open the agent's Carrot site on your phone. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
2. Screen-record while scrolling slowly and smoothly, top to bottom. One pass, 15–20 sec raw.
3. Trim to 10–15 sec. Optional: slightly speed up the middle, keep start/end slow.
4. Phone recording is vertical — perfect. Center it on a navy 1080×1350 canvas (CapCut/Canva) with the agent's name in gold at top.
5. Repeat ×3.

**Homepage screenshots:** desktop browser, full-width, clean crop (no bookmarks bar). Add the gold label bar in Canva.
