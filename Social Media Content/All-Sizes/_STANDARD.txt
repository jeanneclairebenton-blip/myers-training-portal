MYERS SOCIAL POST STANDARD
==========================
Every post is exported in 4 platform sizes, one folder per post:
  • Facebook  1080x1350 (4:5 feed)
  • Instagram 1080x1350 (4:5 feed)
  • LinkedIn  1080x1350 (4:5 feed)
  • Story     1080x1920 (9:16, stories/reels)

Design rules: one focal point, one CTA, high contrast, generous margins,
no font below ~28px on the 1080 canvas (~14pt). 4:5 is the shared feed
standard for FB/IG/LinkedIn; Story is the distinct 9:16 layout.
