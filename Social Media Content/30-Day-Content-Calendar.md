1. **Launch burst:** Days 1–3, also post 1 Story per day (behind-the-scenes, poll, countdown). Stories cost 2 minutes and wake up the algorithm.
2. **Agent amplification — the big one:** every post, 3+ agents share to their personal pages within the first hour. Personal profiles out-reach business pages massively. Make it a Slack ritual: "post is live, share now."
3. **First-hour engagement:** you + 2 agents comment something real on every post within 30 min. Early velocity = distribution.
4. **DM keywords everywhere:** MONDAY, MATH, DEAL, BOTH, JESSE — every reply within 1 hour, every conversation aims at a 15-min call.

## STANDING SERIES: "First Check Club" 🏁

Every time a new agent closes their first deal within their first weeks at Myers, it jumps the queue and takes that week's Tuesday Proof slot. Format:
- Photo of the agent (with their check, at the property, or mid-handshake)
- The timeline is the headline: "Day 1: joined Myers. Day 13: closed her first deal."
- One line on what made it possible (deal flow + mentor — the machine, not luck)
- Close: "Where agents become investors. And where new agents don't wait a year for their first check. DM FIRST."
- Rule: real dates, real deal, agent's permission. This series is only powerful because it's verifiable.

Why it works: it kills the #1 objection to switching/joining — "I'll starve while I ramp." Proof beats promises.
